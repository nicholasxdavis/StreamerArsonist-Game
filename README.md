## Features

- Hit GO LIVE and farm clout
- Health, Sanity, Clout, and Heat bars
- Fake stream chat and donations
- Content from Just Chatting to Subathons
- Arson jobs for clips and heat
- Setup upgrades, from webcam to ISS
- Sponsors, assets, and travel
- Network, raids, and rival beef
- Jail, SWAT raids, and hard mode
- Auto-save
- More!

## Usage

Click the Streamer Arsonist icon to open the side panel. Hit GO LIVE to stream, then use the tabs to pick content, pull arson, upgrade your setup, and manage your network. Progress saves automatically.
